About me

I'm an engineer obsessed with building the next generation of intelligent machines. My work lies at the intersection of robotics, computer vision, and autonomous systems. I believe in a systems-driven approach, where hardware and software are co-designed for optimal performance.

Currently, my focus is on developing robust perception algorithms that allow robots to navigate and interact with complex, unstructured environments. I'm fascinated by the challenge of translating high-level intent into precise physical action.

"Precision is not just a requirement; it is the foundation of autonomy."

Beyond the lab, I'm a student of systems in other forms. I spend my downtime exploring intricate game worlds, analyzing film structures (especially Christopher Nolan's non-linear narratives), playing the piano, and dissecting the world-building in my favorite anime. These pursuits constantly inform my perspective on design and complexity.
